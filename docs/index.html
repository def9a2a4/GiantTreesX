GiantTrees gallery

Every tree the plugin ships, as blocks. Tree types come in up to three sizes: a giant (40–60 blocks tall, 5x5 sapling ring), a huge one (25–40, full 3x3 ring) and a big one (about 14–24, 3x3 plus). Click any picture to open that tree in the viewer, where you can turn it around, cut into it and edit its config.
